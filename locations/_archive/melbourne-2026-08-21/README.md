# Melbourne, built 21 August 2026, archived 2 September 2026

Archived, not deleted, because its composition was derived from the live
Brisbane page on the assumption that Brisbane's structure is why Brisbane
ranks. Three facts checked on 2 September 2026 retired that assumption:
`/location/melbourne` and `/location/adelaide` already exist, both run the
same HubSpot template as Brisbane, and three pages sharing one template do
not share one outcome. So the section list cannot be the ranking variable,
and this page's composition is evidence of a reading rather than of a
result. `locations/PLAN.md` replaces it.

Three things carry forward and are the reason this folder is kept. The
capture contract is settled and correct: both forms carry
`data-location="melbourne"` and never `data-segment`, all six chips are
present with none pre-selected, the group is required, and the suburb field
is prefilled and editable. The two audited Tier 1 proof records survive
intact, Geoff and Wendy, both checked against the decks on 21 August 2026 and
recorded in `paper/proof-register.md` and `shared/locations.json`. And the
checker rules this page proved out stay in `tools/check.mjs` unchanged, so
the next Melbourne page inherits them rather than re-earning them.

Read `COPY.md` here for the copy deck as it stood, including its bracketed
slots. The third proof slot was blocked on the client then and still is, and
its state is tracked in `shared/locations.json` under
`melbourneThirdProofContested` rather than here.
