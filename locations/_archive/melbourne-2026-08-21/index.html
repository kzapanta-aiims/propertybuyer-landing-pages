Access

Off market access in Melbourne

Off market means seeing properties before they are advertised, through relationships with selling agents built over more than two decades. It is access, not a discount: off market stock is not automatically cheaper, and anyone who promises otherwise is selling something. What it buys you is choice, and time to act before the crowd arrives.
