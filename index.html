Questions we get from home buyers

What does a buyer's agent do?

We find the property, inspect it, tell you what it is worth, and negotiate or bid for you. You keep the decision. We do the work and carry the risk of getting it wrong.

How long does it usually take?

Most clients go from engagement to purchase in 30 to 60 days. A tight brief in a thin market takes longer, and we will say so at the start rather than at the end.

Does the seller pay you anything?

No. We act for buyers only and we have never sold a property, so there is no commission coming to us from the other side of the table and no reason to prefer one listing over another.

Can you bid at auction for me?

Yes. We set the limit with you beforehand, then we bid. The selling agent reads the room during an auction, and reading you is part of that. It is easier when you are not in it.

I have already found a property.

We can value it and negotiate on it without running the search. Tell us the address when you get in touch and we will say whether it is worth what they are asking.
