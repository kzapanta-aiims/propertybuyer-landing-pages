Questions
we get from commercial buyers

What does a commercial buyer's agent do?

We find the property, inspect it, tell you what it is worth, and negotiate or bid for you. You keep the decision. We do the work and carry the risk of getting it wrong.

Should I buy or keep leasing?

That depends on what the lease costs you, what the premises would cost to own, and how long you plan to stay. We put the two side by side with real numbers before you commit to either.

How long does it usually take?

Most clients go from engagement to purchase in 30 to 60 days. A tight requirement in a liquid market can be faster; a rare one takes as long as it takes to find the right building.

Does the vendor pay you anything?

No. We take no commission from vendors, developers or agents. You are the only person paying us, so you are the only person we answer to.

Can you buy in a state I am not in?

Yes. We have offices in Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane and Adelaide, and we inspect in person so you do not have to fly.
